Formation gates

A possible domain is not yet a Maison.

New Houses are formed only after the flagship compounds and a real category, operator, capability, demand and parent-preserving economic structure exist.

Distinctive category

A clear, ownable mandate that cannot be reduced to a GoalOS feature or research programme.

Exceptional operator

A human, machine or hybrid leader capable of autonomy inside the parent constitution.

Rights-cleared capability

Proprietary or licensed capability whose provenance, scope, limits and revocation are explicit.

Premium demand

A responsible customer, budget and evidence burden large enough to justify institution formation.

Positive contribution economics

Value after delivery, validation, liability, security and continuing support—not vanity revenue.
