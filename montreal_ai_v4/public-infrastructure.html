ENS identity estate

AI → AGI → ASI as durable public naming.

agi.eth and asi.eth are presented as roots for role legibility, routing, reputation and controlled scale. A name does not, by itself, prove competence, authority or acceptance.

Canonical grammar

<entity>.(<env>.)<role>.agi.eth
role ∈ {club, agent, node}
env ∈ ENV_SET

Examples: alice.club.agi.eth · helper.agent.agi.eth · gpu01.node.agi.eth.

Scope law

entity.role.agi.eth is a global role identity. entity.env.role.agi.eth is environment-scoped and recognized only within the applicable env.agi.eth policy.

Operational authority remains separately credentialed, scoped and revocable.
