# Independent counsel review required

This release is a legal-hardening and regulatory-minimization architecture, not a legal opinion and not a guarantee that no regulator, claimant, customer, worker, user or third party will raise an issue.

Before live commercialization, obtain Québec counsel review of:

- the final live pages and general impression;
- French-language parity and contractual language process;
- the Publisher identity and corporate disclosures;
- the Terms acceptance mechanism;
- any token, protocol, validator, payment, custody, settlement or fundraising activity;
- any private data collection, AI interaction, customer deployment or cross-border transfer;
- any consumer sale, subscription or distance contract;
- any regulated professional, employment, credit, insurance, health, public-sector, critical-infrastructure or security workflow;
- any performance, ROI, customer, validator, security, mainnet or external-validation claim.

Mandatory law and actual facts prevail over labels and disclaimers.
