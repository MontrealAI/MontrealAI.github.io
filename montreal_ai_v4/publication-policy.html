Public Publication Policy

What may be public, what requires separate review, and what must remain protected.

Version1.0
Effective1 August 2026
Governing postureMandatory law and actual facts prevail

Seven publication gates

  1. Rights: ownership, licences, attribution and third-party material are confirmed.
  2. Claims: each material statement is classified and scoped.
  3. Regulatory: no unreviewed offer, token, investment, return, payment, custody or professional-service claim appears.
  4. Privacy and security: no personal information, customer evidence, privilege, secrets, keys or exploit-sensitive data appears.
  5. Evidence: simulations, public records, independent review, customer acceptance and realized outcomes remain distinct.
  6. Versioning: release ID, effective date, checksum, manifest and supersession are recorded.
  7. Approval: rights, claims, privacy, security and publication owners approve the release.

Public class

High-level doctrine, public-safe research, status records, source citations, browser-local demonstrations, release manifests and narrow infrastructure evidence may be public when the gates pass.

Separate-review class

Token economics, fundraising, pricing, customer proposals, partnership terms, investment or value communications, private deployment architecture, professional opinions and regulated-sector materials require specific review.

Protected class

Customer evidence, private publisher vaults, signing material, credentials, recovery routes, personal information, privileged advice, incident records, unpublished inventions, trade secrets and exploit-sensitive details must not enter the public repository or GitHub Pages package.

Corrections and withdrawal

The Publisher may correct, narrow, archive, supersede or withdraw any Material. A correction record should preserve the reason and version history when practical.

Publication law. A public artifact must be accurate enough to read without a private oral qualification.
