Executive definition

The crown jewel is not the model. It is the institution.

Models can be substituted. Interfaces and workflows can be copied. The durable moat is the rights-governed graph of customers, missions, evidence, evaluators, methods, failures, operating history, trusted capabilities, infrastructure and capital-allocation competence.

Not money by magic

No promise of revenue, profit, valuation, autonomous success or guaranteed outcome. Every commercial claim remains contingent on real buyers, real payment, bounded delivery and accountable acceptance.

Not sales automation

The institution governs discovery, consent, qualification, agreement, delivery, proof, renewal, Chronicle admission and reinvestment as distinct states.

Not self-certified

GoalOS may execute and reason deeply. It never becomes its own final judge. Independent challenge and responsible customer acceptance remain separate.

Proof-and-settlement division

Four layers. No confused authority.

Cryptography can establish what was committed or recorded. It cannot independently establish that an off-chain proposition was true.

MONTREAL.AI

Builds and governs the proof architecture, constitutional boundaries, Chronicle rules, background IP, quality standards and customer relationship.

Token layer

May activate crypto-native validators, operators, collectors and patrons through bounded incentives. It does not own MONTREAL.AI, control GoalOS or define truth.

Blockchain layer

Records commitments, identities, stakes, verdicts, disputes, attestations and settlement within the declared protocol scope.

Responsible authority

The customer or legally competent authority retains final consequential acceptance and may dispute, condition, reject or stop the mission.

Independent validators

Challenge material claims under conflict, competence, evidence and replay rules. Validation is not customer acceptance.

Chronicle

Decides what accepted, attributable, scoped, rights-cleared and revocable capability may influence future missions.

Constitutional separation. Execute ≠ Validate ≠ Accept ≠ Admit ≠ Settle. No role silently inherits the authority of another.

Token infrastructure

Utility for validation—not ownership or truth.

A pilot may use $AGIALPHA where appropriate, testnet settlement, non-transferable validator credentials, internal accounting points or conventional escrow with on-chain evidence commitments.

The token may support

  • payment for validated AGI Jobs;
  • validator bonds or stakes;
  • rewards for independent verification;
  • penalties or withheld rewards for provably defective participation;
  • dispute deposits and protocol fees;
  • mission or validator tiers;
  • integrity-preserving settlement;
  • reputation signals linked to completed work.

The token does not provide

  • ownership or control of MONTREAL.AI or GoalOS;
  • equity, dividends, profit sharing or guaranteed liquidity;
  • automatic validator competence or independence;
  • truth about an off-chain event;
  • customer acceptance or regulated authority;
  • permission to bypass contracts, law, privacy or safety boundaries.
