# Deployment guide

1. Extract the publish-ready ZIP.
2. Upload the **contents** to the root of `MontrealAI/MontrealAI.github.io` through a reviewed branch.
3. Preserve unrelated historical application routes.
4. Let the packaged validation workflow complete.
5. Verify `/`, `/fr/`, `/goalos.html`, `/fr/goalos.html`, `/commission.html`, `/fr/commission.html`, `/legal.html` and `/fr/legal.html`.
6. Test EN/FR from desktop and mobile navigation.
7. Configure `montreal.ai` separately.
8. Complete final live Safari, Firefox, iOS, Android, accessibility and Québec legal-language review.
