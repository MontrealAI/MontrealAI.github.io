# MONTREAL.AI Regulatory Fortress v124.0.0

## Objective

Reduce avoidable legal and regulatory exposure of the public MONTREAL.AI GitHub Pages institution through architectural exclusion, bilingual notice, claim discipline, privacy minimization, public/private separation, governed interactive access and release evidence.

## Principal controls

1. No checkout, payment, token sale, exchange, custody, remittance, staking, lending or wallet request on the parent public site.
2. No server-side mission intake, analytics, advertising, account or newsletter.
3. Full English/French legal center and Terms.
4. French Terms supplied first in the English affirmative-acceptance gate.
5. One-time in-memory legal gate for interactive demos, package downloads and local institutional tools.
6. Full Terms adapted from the supplied GoalOS Sovereign Reserve legal model.
7. Separate token/blockchain, AI-transparency, institutional-status, claims, publication, rights, privacy and security notices.
8. Public/private exclusion register and prohibition on publishing Publisher Vaults, credentials or customer evidence.
9. Restrictive Content Security Policy and no-referrer posture.
10. Commission email minimized to a request for an approved protected intake path.

## Residual risk

No website terms can bind regulators or non-signatories, waive mandatory law, alter actual conduct or guarantee immunity. Counsel review remains required.
