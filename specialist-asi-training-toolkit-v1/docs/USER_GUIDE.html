User guide

1. Access

Choose one equal eligibility route. The app reads current Mainnet state, creates a short-lived domain-bound receipt and revalidates periodically. Access never creates mission authority.

2. Command Center

The Command Center shows the complete progression, current mission, proof state, Foundry generation, authority posture and Chronicle depth.

3. Navigator Ω

Record capability, cost, market, law, security, infrastructure, provider and evidence changes. Compare adaptation lag with capability half-life through the Singularity Exposure Ratio.

4. Successor Book

Maintain incumbent, immediate, challenger, reserve, hedge and retirement positions. Move candidate architectures between positions as evidence changes.

5. Mission Constitution

Freeze the exact mission, beneficiary, incumbent, reference alternatives, critical failures, constraints, proof budget and authority ceiling.

6. Successor Manifold

Compare complete architectures rather than model names. Every candidate includes intelligence, data, tools, verifier, workflow, governance, portability, resilience, burden and human roles.

7. Mission Advantage Gradient

Identify which bounded architecture change is expected to increase proof-adjusted mission value most efficiently.

8. SEIZE Underwriting

Price the proof opportunity, downside, formation cost, duration and option value. SEIZE authorizes only the named proof programme.

9. AGI Jobs

Compile the canonical 21 work-and-proof contracts. Each job has a bounded objective, output, evidence requirement, verifier and rollback route.

10. Verifier Foundry

Build the independent outcome, constraint, evidence, process, economics, transfer and anti-gaming verifiers before scaling candidate training. Development evaluators may teach; the authoritative verifier remains outside candidate control.

11. Experience and Curriculum Foundry

Assemble rights-cleared historical replay, expert demonstrations, synthetic cases, adversarial cases, rare failures, distribution shifts and Chronicle failure replay. Protected fresh cases never enter this curriculum.

12. Formation Gym

Run matched seeded cases against the incumbent and competing architectures. Formation is visible, iterative and adaptive.

13. Training Toolkit Ω

Route the mission to deterministic, offline, agentic, world-model, neuro-symbolic, coordination and evolutionary candidate families. Run the protected cycle, review the exact release commitment and compare proof-adjusted Mission Alpha.

14. Recursive Foundry

Generate bounded descendants, preserve quality diversity, reject critical and governance regressions, and freeze only one exact challenger.

15. Fresh Proof

Evaluate the frozen candidate on protected representative work. Passing the local simulation is not a real-world Specialist ASI claim.

16. Specialist ASI

Inspect capability, economics, reliability, sovereignty, governance and transfer gates. Specialist ASI is earned, not declared.

17. Authority

Select only an authority level justified by current proof and the mission ceiling. Run stress tests and preserve rollback.

18. Chronicle and Requalification

Record accepted capability, limitations, failures, authority changes and successors. Material changes remove inherited proof and require a new release.

19. AI Studio

Choose:

  • offline deterministic compiler;
  • secure AI backend;
  • ChatGPT Bridge.

AI may propose and compile. It cannot create fresh proof or grant authority.

20. Evidence Room

Attach local text, CSV, JSON or Markdown evidence. Files remain local unless explicitly included in a secure AI request.

21. Export

Download the project, environment, candidate manifest, proof report, Authority Envelope, Chronicle, recursive report, Board Brief and complete Mission Pack.
