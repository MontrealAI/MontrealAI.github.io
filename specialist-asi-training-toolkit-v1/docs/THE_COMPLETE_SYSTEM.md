# The Complete Verifier-First Institution

Architecture


 The Toolkit does not merely train an AI. It determines whether a mission is trainable, whether its rewards are trustworthy, which complete institution should compete, whether one frozen challenger truly wins, what authority it deserves—and when it must be replaced. 
 The governing hierarchy  Mission Constitution Governs purpose, scope, rights, hard constraints, acceptance and prohibited actions.  Verifier Governs reward grounding, evidence, false acceptance and anti-gaming.  Foundry Governs algorithm routing and competition among complete institutions.  Fresh Proof Governs promotion of one exact frozen challenger.  Accountable Authority Governs admission, scope, expiry, rollback, revocation and requalification.  Chronicle Preserves only evidenced, rights-cleared, versioned and challengeable capability.
