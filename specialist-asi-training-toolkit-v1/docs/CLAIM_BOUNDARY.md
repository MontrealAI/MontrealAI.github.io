# Claim boundary

GoalOS v9.0.0-UVSI3 is a production-quality synthetic reference implementation and protected-backend architecture. It demonstrates the institutional mechanics required to form, freeze, compare and govern candidate successor architectures.

It does **not** claim that:

- a universal ASI exists;
- any external system has earned Specialist ASI status;
- any candidate has production authority;
- the reference “Lite” algorithms reproduce frontier-scale neural implementations;
- the synthetic transition models faithfully represent a customer’s production reality;
- wallet eligibility establishes professional, corporate, financial or mission authority;
- a high Gym score establishes real-world value.

A real Specialist ASI claim requires an exact release, protected fresh representative work, independent evidence custody, complete cost and intervention accounting, hard-gate passage, matched comparison against the incumbent and best credible alternative, independent review and accountable acceptance.

A Mission-Sovereign Successor Ω requires a separate accountable admission, exact mission scope, Authority Envelope, monitoring, expiry, rollback, revocation and requalification.
