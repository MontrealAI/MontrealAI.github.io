# MONTREAL.AI Public-Site Legal Hardening Architecture — v11.0.0

## Objective

Reduce avoidable public-site exposure through architectural exclusion, bilingual notice, claim discipline, privacy minimization, public/private separation, governed interactive access and versioned release evidence.

## Principal controls

1. No checkout, payment, token sale, exchange, custody, remittance, staking, lending or wallet request on the parent public site.
2. No server-side mission intake, analytics, advertising, account or newsletter.
3. Complete English/French legal estate and exact reciprocal route parity.
4. Browser-local tools are identified as drafts, simulations or reference implementations and do not grant production authority.
5. Separate token/blockchain, AI-transparency, institutional-status, claims, publication, rights, privacy and security notices.
6. Public/private exclusion register prohibiting credentials, private vaults, personal information, customer evidence, privileged materials and trade secrets.
7. Restrictive Content Security Policy and no-referrer posture within the limits of static hosting.
8. Commission email is minimized to a request for an approved protected intake path.
9. Shared-header contrast and mobile-navigation controls are independently tested across the complete legal estate.
10. Research, implementation, demonstration, validation, acceptance, admission, settlement and realized outcomes remain distinct claim classes.

## Residual boundary

This architecture is not a legal opinion. Terms and notices cannot bind regulators or non-signatories, waive mandatory law, change actual conduct or guarantee immunity. Actual facts, deployment configuration and applicable law prevail.
