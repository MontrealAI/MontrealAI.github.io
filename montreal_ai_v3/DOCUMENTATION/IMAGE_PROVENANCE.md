# Image provenance

The production website uses the supplied and inherited institutional artwork and the authentic existing founder photograph. No synthetic founder likeness is used.

Six generated concept boards are included in `CONCEPT_BOARDS/`. They were used as visual direction for hierarchy, contrast, information density, solar-gold energy and institutional coherence. They are not deployed as fake screenshots or substituted for accessible HTML. The production pages are real semantic HTML/CSS/JavaScript interfaces.

Illustrative architectural imagery is explicitly disclosed where it could otherwise be mistaken for a physical facility.
