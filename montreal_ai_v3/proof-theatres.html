Truth boundary

What theatre can and cannot prove.

A demonstration can prove that its local code follows a state transition, detects tamper or produces a reproducible commitment. It does not prove mainnet deployment, external performance, a customer, payment or off-chain event.

Can prove

Code, states, calculations, artifacts, hashes and reproducible behavior in the published version.

Can demonstrate

Reference architecture, roles, gates, expected reverts, revocation and rollback.

Cannot prove

Off-chain truth, independent validation, customer outcomes, live funds or production authority.

Next step

Real mission, frozen contract, external execution, docket and responsible acceptance.
