# GoalOS Singularity Office Ω — Operating Runbook

## Continuous

- monitor source-pack expiry, mission state, proof expiry, authority expiry and resilience signals;
- block dependent actions when required evidence or authority is absent;
- preserve the decision ledger and encrypted Evidence Vault under user control.

## Weekly

- review Frontier Radar changes;
- update the Value-of-Proof queue;
- advance, block or retire bounded missions;
- issue the Weekly Frontier and Institution Brief.

## Monthly

- run the Architecture Auction;
- review the Institutional Capability Twin;
- review verified value, externalities and capital allocation;
- revalidate authority and proof posture.

## Quarterly

- reconstitute the institution against all twelve architectures;
- compare the incumbent, successor and reserve option;
- run counterfactual stress worlds;
- update switch, stop and rollback conditions.

## Material event

Trigger immediate review after a major capability release, source change, security event, financing, acquisition, regulatory change, ownership change, critical-provider failure or infrastructure commitment.

## Annual

- complete authority, privacy, security, continuity and provider reviews;
- rotate publisher keys where required;
- review Chronicle admissions, expiries and revocations;
- document the next-year capital-to-capacity constitution.
