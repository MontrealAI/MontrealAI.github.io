# Claim Boundary — GoalOS Singularity Office Ω v2.0.0-SO2

## What this release is

A complete, bilingual, browser-local institutional-control application supporting:

- mission constitution and portfolio governance;
- twelve competing institutional architectures;
- deterministic counterfactual stress testing;
- twelve transparent council roles;
- twenty-one bounded AGI Job templates;
- fifteen proof and authority receipts;
- Institutional Capability Twin;
- model and execution-stack auction;
- Evidence Vault and decision ledger;
- Chronicle admission and fresh-successor comparison;
- verified-value and capital-to-capacity ledgers;
- six-domain monitoring and operating cadence;
- board-ready reporting;
- AGI Club and signed institutional-licence access.

## What it is not

It does not itself provide legal, tax, investment, medical or other regulated professional advice; government approval; a securities offering; custody; external transaction execution; confidential server-side DRM; guaranteed funding, performance or compliance; or proof that a represented external outcome occurred.

## Algorithmic-output notice

Scenarios, scores, agent-role deliberations and recommendations are generated by software. They are candidate institutional designs. Material decisions require current facts, current official sources, independent review where appropriate, accountable acceptance and applicable professional authority.

## Recursive-improvement boundary

Chronicle and the Successor Laboratory implement the discipline and records required to test improvement. An improvement claim requires a fresh comparable mission, preregistered metrics, evidence of the result and no material governance regression.
