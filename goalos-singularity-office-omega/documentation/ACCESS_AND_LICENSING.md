# Access and Licensing Architecture

## AGI Club access

Ordinary member use may be granted to a connected Ethereum Mainnet wallet that currently and directly owns one exact one-label `label.club.agi.eth` name. Resolver control, approvals, operator status, management authority, former ownership and beneficial assertions do not qualify.

## Institutional licences

Signed licences may authorize Professional, Practitioner, Team, Institution, Protected Deployment or Sovereign tiers. The browser validates issuer signature, product, release major, dates, allowed origins and licence fields against the publisher public key.

## Publisher key custody

The publisher private signing key is not included in the public deployment or the public complete suite. It is delivered only in a separate PRIVATE Publisher Vault. Keep it encrypted offline, restrict operator access and rotate immediately if compromise is suspected.

## Public-static boundary

GitHub Pages publishes client-side source. Access controls support membership qualification, provenance and conditional-use licensing; they do not create source secrecy or unbypassable server-side DRM.
