Talent and Maison leadership

Exceptional leaders receive real autonomy with earned rights.

MONTREAL.AI recruits operators capable of defining categories—not managers of undifferentiated projects. Project participation belongs below the parent and vests through attributable contribution.

01

Institutional roles

Craftsmanship for sovereign intelligence.

Maison leader

Own mission, P&L, team, customer execution and culture inside a revocable charter.

Mission architect

Translate objectives into Mission Contracts, authority, evidence and acceptance tests.

Authority engineer

Map principals, delegated powers, tools, budgets, prohibitions, expiry and rollback.

Evidence curator

Build inspectable Dockets with provenance, tests, failures, interventions and rights.

Independent validator

Challenge evidence without controlling customer acceptance or Chronicle admission.

Chronicle steward

Govern rights-cleared capability, expiry, retirement, revocation and transfer.

Lead a Maison

Autonomy is meaningful.

  • Genuine operating mandate
  • Approved capital envelope
  • Project-level compensation and milestone vesting

  • MONTREAL.AI ownership, brand and background IP
  • Constitutional standards and public claims
  • Material capital and change-of-control decisions
