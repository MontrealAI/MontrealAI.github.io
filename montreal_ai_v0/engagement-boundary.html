No public-site engagement. Reading the site, generating a local brief, sending an email or discussing a mission does not create a contract, reserve capacity, authorize work, establish confidentiality or require acceptance.

Public-safe inquiry only

Initial inquiries must contain no confidential, privileged, personal, regulated, classified, credential, security-sensitive or unauthorized third-party information. MONTREAL.AI may decline or delete unsolicited sensitive material.

When an engagement begins

A customer engagement begins only through a separate written instrument signed by authorized representatives that defines the parties, mission, consideration, authority, data, intellectual property, evidence, acceptance, security, privacy, professional roles, liability, change control and termination.

No automatic execution

The public site does not collect payment, connect a wallet, form an entity, file with government, contract with a third party, move funds, make regulated decisions about individuals, deploy into production or bind MONTREAL.AI.

Professional and regulated work

Where work requires legal, accounting, tax, medical, engineering, securities, insurance, employment, cybersecurity, government or other regulated judgment, the required qualified professional or responsible authority must remain in control and issue the relevant decision.

Selective distribution

MONTREAL.AI may accept, condition, defer or refuse any proposed mission. No public availability, listed price, generated document or discussion creates a duty to contract or a guarantee of capacity.

Public boundary. Public institutional publication. Experimental research and reference implementations. No offer, advice, token sale, custody, payment service, certification, authority grant or outcome guarantee. Interactive systems are simulations or public references unless a deployment-specific notice says otherwise.
