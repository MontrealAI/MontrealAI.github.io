The trust grammar

Not all public artifacts prove the same thing.

The Proof Room classifies deployed systems, research publications, mainnet identifiers, verification records, customer acceptance, realized outcomes and Chronicle-admitted capability separately.

Deployed public system

Evidence that an interface or application exists under a stated release.

Research publication

Evidence that a doctrine or architecture has been articulated—not that it has produced an accepted outcome.

Mainnet identifier

Evidence that a contract, address or transaction exists on-chain.

Verification record

Evidence that a defined test, package or release control was executed.

Institutional acceptance

Evidence that a responsible external institution accepted a bounded result.

Chronicle admission

Evidence that accepted, rights-cleared capability passed transfer and may influence a successor.

Evidence Docket 000

Mainnet procurement infrastructure proof

A narrow public record can establish that an identified premium procurement and settlement job was constituted through the referenced infrastructure.

It does not establish: future off-chain work quality, truth, customer acceptance, realized economic value or suitability for another deployment.
