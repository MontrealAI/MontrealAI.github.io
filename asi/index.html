The constitutional threshold

Continuity passes. Authority does not.

A successor is an institutionally qualified continuation—not merely a copied agent or newer checkpoint. The inheritance is broad; the authority grant is fresh, bounded and independently evidenced.

MCAP-001 conformance

Recognition must be earned in stages.

Namespace ownership can make an institution discoverable. It cannot self-certify operational legitimacy.

Level 1

Discoverable

Root, manifest, Covenant, registry and public status can be resolved.

Level 2

Auditable

Chronicle commitments, proof references, controllers, conflicts and succession history are public.

Level 3

Operational

A frozen candidate completes independent proof, receives bounded admission, and demonstrates monitoring and rollback.
