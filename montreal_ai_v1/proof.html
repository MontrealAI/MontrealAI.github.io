Constitutional separation

Execute ≠ Validate ≠ Accept ≠ Admit ≠ Settle

Cryptography can establish what was signed, committed, recorded or settled. It cannot independently determine whether an off-chain proposition is true or responsibly accepted.

Execute

Agents and human operators perform bounded work.

Validate

Independent reviewers challenge material claims and evidence.

Accept

The responsible customer or authority decides whether the result may be relied upon.

Admit

Chronicle decides whether accepted, rights-cleared capability may influence future missions.

Settle

Contractual and blockchain systems record commitments, disputes and economic consequences.

Revoke

Authority, credentials, records and capability remain versioned and revocable.
