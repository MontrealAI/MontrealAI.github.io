Qualification boundary

The right objective has consequences.

A Sprint should begin only when its proof can materially change a real decision.

Strong fit

  • Responsible executive or institutional sponsor
  • Approved budget and decision deadline
  • One workflow with meaningful value at stake
  • Measurable cost of error or unsupported output
  • Willingness to accept CONDITION, REPAIR, REJECT, PIVOT or STOP

Do not proceed yet

  • No accountable acceptance authority
  • No access to required evidence or subject-matter review
  • Objective requires hidden, unlawful or unauthorized activity
  • Success can only be declared by the system selling the result
  • The desired outcome is predetermined regardless of evidence
