Claims & Evidence Policy

How MONTREAL.AI separates architecture, implementation, simulation, public record, independent review, customer acceptance and realized outcomes.

Claim discipline is part of the institution. Ambition may be large; the evidence class must remain exact.

Effective1 August 2026
Governing postureMandatory law and actual facts prevail

Evidence taxonomy

SpecifiedA doctrine, standard or architecture has been written.Implementation, safety, acceptance or outcome.

Restricted claims

Do not publish or imply “fully protected,” “regulation-free,” “100% safe,” “risk-free,” “guaranteed,” “approved,” “certified,” “independently validated,” “production ready,” “self-improving,” “autonomous,” “live,” “mainnet,” “verified,” “accepted,” “revenue producing,” “Type I,” “AGI” or “ASI” without a nearby scope, status, date, evidence and limitation that make the general impression accurate.

Illustrative economics

ROI, value-at-stake, price architecture, market size, capacity and portfolio figures must be labelled as illustrative models, strategic objectives or scenarios unless they are realized, reconciled and supported by a public-safe evidence record. No modeled figure is a return promise or investment forecast.

Visual claims

Illustrations must not create a false impression of a physical headquarters, customer, government affiliation, operational network or certified facility. AI-assisted institutional artwork is labelled as illustrative.

Corrections

A materially inaccurate or stale claim must be corrected, narrowed, archived or withdrawn. Corrections should preserve the prior version, reason, date and responsible editor where practical.

Public rule. Describe what exists. State what it proves. State what it does not prove. Let responsible acceptance remain external.
