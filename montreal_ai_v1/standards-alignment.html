Alignment is not certification. References to laws, frameworks, standards, guidelines or institutions describe design considerations only unless an identified authority has formally reviewed and approved the exact scope.

Design-alignment posture

The architecture is intended to support documented authority, risk classification, human oversight, evidence, traceability, security, privacy, challenge, rollback, change control and post-deployment monitoring. This does not establish compliance with any law or standard.

Deployment-specific determination

Applicable obligations depend on the actual system, intended purpose, data, affected people, customer role, geography, level of autonomy, provider/deployer allocation and consequences. A public reference architecture cannot pre-classify every deployment.

No external endorsement

No regulator, standards body, government, university, model provider, blockchain network or cited institution is represented as endorsing MONTREAL.AI or GoalOS merely because its material is linked, discussed or used as a design reference.

Review rule

Before regulated or high-impact use, the responsible institution must conduct the appropriate legal, privacy, security, human-rights, professional and sector-specific assessments and obtain any required authorization.

Public boundary. Public institutional publication. Experimental research and reference implementations. No offer, advice, token sale, custody, payment service, certification, authority grant or outcome guarantee. Interactive systems are simulations or public references unless a deployment-specific notice says otherwise.
