GoalOS · Proof-governed economic value

La valeur de l’IA n’est pas la sortie. C’est la qualité de la décision qu’elle rend possible.

GoalOS rend la thèse économique explicite, inspectable, modifiable, mesurable et falsifiable avant que la confiance mérite une autorité institutionnelle.

La règle de qualification

Tous les objectifs ne méritent pas GoalOS.

The right objective has a real executive sponsor, an approved budget, a hard decision deadline, an explicit proof burden, a measurable cost of error and the institutional courage to condition, repair, reject, pivot—or stop.

THE ECONOMIC LAW

La preuve est une infrastructure lorsqu’elle peut changer l’action optimale.

The mission is justified when the expected value of better evidence materially exceeds the cost, delay and risk of obtaining it.

Trois cas d’usage conséquents

La thèse économique mérite l’attention. L’Evidence Docket mérite l’autorité.

USE CASE 01

Enterprise AI deployment assurance

A modeled $6M rollout, $100K proof investment and $801K gross economic value.

  • production tests;
  • hallucination and abstention;
  • security and data leakage;
  • monitoring and rollback;
  • conditional deployment decision.

701% modeled ROI.

USE CASE 02

Capital allocation and acquisition diligence

A modeled $30M commitment, $200K proof investment and $1.55M gross economic value.

  • strategic claims;
  • valuation assumptions;
  • integration risk;
  • contractual protections;
  • board-ready contradictions.

677% modeled ROI.

USE CASE 03

Three-customer commercial proof run

A modeled $125K total investment and $461K probability-weighted gross value.

  • one bounded offer;
  • three real paying customers;
  • repeat-purchase test;
  • delivery economics;
  • stop before additional GTM burn.

269% modeled ROI.
