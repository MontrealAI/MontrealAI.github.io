La capsule de capacité

Preuve publique. Capacité privée. Autorité gouvernée.

A model can be substituted. A workflow can be copied. The accumulated graph of missions, evidence, evaluators, rights, failures, methods and trusted capability cannot be reproduced overnight.

Architecture boundary. A public Merkle root or policy proof can establish commitment and integrity. It does not independently prove that an off-chain result was true, accepted or suitable for reuse.

01

Ce à quoi le graphe doit répondre

La mémoire est une décision gouvernée—pas une transcription.

PROOF

What actually worked?

Which claims passed, which evidence supports them, and which failures or interventions remain material?

AUTHORITY

Who validated it?

Which reviewer, policy, acceptance authority and conflict rule governed the result?

SCOPE

Where may it be reused?

What environment, data class, mission type, expiry, cost and risk boundary applies?

RIGHTS

Who may invoke it?

Identity, entitlement, licence, revocation and protected execution remain explicit.

FORGETTING

What must not survive?

Unproved, expired, contradicted, unsafe or rights-defective capability is repaired, quarantined, revoked or forgotten.

SUCCESSION

Did the next generation improve?

A fresh mission under equal or tighter constraints must show superior performance.

Loi canonique de capacité privée

L’agent reçoit un droit d’invocation gouverné. MONTREAL.AI conserve l’intelligence.

The proposed private-capability network admits only current, direct identities with valid, scoped and revocable entitlements. Invocation is governed; transfer of the underlying private intelligence is not implied.

NO AUTOMATIC ADMISSION

Capability must earn survival.

  • accepted mission result;
  • independent validation;
  • rights clearance;
  • known limitations;
  • version and expiry;
  • fresh-task transfer;
  • revocation and rollback.
