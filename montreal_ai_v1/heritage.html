AI → AGI → ASI as durable public naming.

AGI.eth and ASI.eth support identity, role legibility, routing and controlled scale. Direct subnames can represent agents, validators, nodes and project-specific institutions under explicit scope.

Canonical grammar

<entity>.(<env>.)<role>.agi.eth
role ∈ {club, agent, node}
env ∈ ENV_SET

Examples: alice.club.agi.eth · helper.agent.agi.eth · gpu01.node.agi.eth
