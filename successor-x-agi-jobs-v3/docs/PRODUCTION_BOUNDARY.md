# Production Boundary

A production Successor Ω is not created by deploying this repository.

Production requires, at minimum:

- one rights-cleared, consequential and measurable mission;
- authenticated organizational roles and separation of powers;
- confidential evidence storage and retention policy;
- protected proof cases under independent custody;
- secure candidate, scorer and release identities;
- an independently reviewed Authority Envelope;
- external least-privilege gateways and scoped credentials;
- human and professional approval where required;
- continuous monitoring, drift and evidence-expiry detection;
- incident response, revocation and a tested known-good fallback;
- new proof and admission after material change;
- security review of smart contracts and operational services.

The package controls no physical system, releases no funds and grants no organizational authority by default.
