# Verifier Foundry

The Verifier Foundry is the most important training asset because autonomous improvement depends on gradeable experience whose reward remains tied to real mission success.

## Verifier classes

- **Outcome:** did the accepted mission result occur?
- **Constitutional constraints:** were critical errors, safety, rights, privacy, budget and authority limits preserved?
- **Evidence:** does every material conclusion carry sufficient attributable support?
- **Process:** were required intermediate gates, tools, interventions and escalations followed?
- **Complete denominator:** were compute, services, human rescue, maintenance, requalification and unwind counted?
- **Transfer:** does the capability survive new regimes and related missions?
- **Anti-gaming:** can the candidate exploit the scorer, leak protected cases, hide interventions or collude with evaluators?

## Separation

Development verifiers may guide formation. The authoritative Fresh-Proof Verifier is independently governed and unavailable to candidate-controlled coordinators. The candidate may cross the proof firewall; protected information may not return.
