# Training guide

## 1. Select the mission before the algorithm

Begin with one valuable, repeatable, bounded and verifiable mission. Freeze the incumbent, best credible alternatives, hard constraints, complete denominator, proof budget, authority ceiling and stop-loss.

## 2. Build the verifier before scaling training

Do not train an ambitious candidate where a high reward can be obtained without solving the mission. Run the verifier adversarial audit and establish false-acceptance, false-rejection and calibration targets.

## 3. Compile the curriculum

Use ordinary cases, ambiguity, rare failures, adversarial attacks, distribution shifts and transfer preparation. Keep protected cases outside formation.

## 4. Route the portfolio

- formal rules: deterministic and DUPLEX-style candidates;
- historical logs: behaviour cloning, IQL and CQL;
- multi-turn tool use: AT²PO-style policies;
- hidden delayed dynamics: MuZero or Dreamer-style world models;
- specialist coordination: TRINITY, AsyncThink and multi-agent learning;
- open architecture uncertainty: quality diversity, OpenEvolve and lineage search.

## 5. Train complete institutions

The object of competition includes policy, models, data, tools, workflow, verifier interface, human roles, rollback and authority boundary. Algorithms train components; the Foundry competes institutions.

## 6. Freeze and prove

Freeze models, weights, prompts, tools, data, workflow, verifier, budget, permissions and rollback. Run one-way fresh proof. Select only candidates that pass every hard gate and produce positive conservative Mission Alpha.

## 7. Admit separately

A synthetic or real capability result creates no authority. Accountable principals define the exact Authority Envelope, expiry, monitoring and rollback.
