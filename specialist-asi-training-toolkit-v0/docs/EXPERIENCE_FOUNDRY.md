# Experience and Curriculum Foundry

The Experience Foundry manufactures attributable mission experience in six graduated stages:

1. ordinary operations;
2. ambiguity, abstention and evidence acquisition;
3. rare failure replay;
4. adversarial challenge;
5. provider, regime and distribution shift;
6. transfer preparation.

Protected fresh-proof and transfer stages are generated from separate seed commitments and are never visible during formation.

## Admissible sources

- rights-cleared historical episodes;
- expert demonstrations;
- synthetic task generation with independently knowable answers;
- self-play where the reward remains grounded;
- adversarial cases and authority traps;
- Chronicle failure replay.

A task generator may propose tasks, answers or validators. It may not authorize them for training or promotion. Mission relevance, rights, answer soundness, verifier robustness and blind-set separation remain independent gates.
