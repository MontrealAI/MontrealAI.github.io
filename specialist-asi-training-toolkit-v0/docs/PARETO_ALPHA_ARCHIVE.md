# Proof-Adjusted Pareto Alpha Archive

One scalar score is not allowed to erase institutional diversity.

The archive evaluates complete candidate institutions across:

- conservative Mission Alpha;
- fresh quality;
- transfer;
- evidence coverage;
- sovereignty;
- governance;
- complete cost;
- human burden;
- complexity;
- Gym-to-reality basis risk.

The non-dominated frontier and distinct elites are retained even when they are not the current champion. This preserves useful reserves, hedges, stepping stones and safer fallbacks.
