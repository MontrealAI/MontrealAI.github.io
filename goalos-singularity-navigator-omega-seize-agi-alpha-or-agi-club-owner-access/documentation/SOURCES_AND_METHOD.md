# Sources and Method

The embedded frontier snapshot is dated 26 July 2026 and is structured around official materials from OpenAI, NIST, the European Commission, W3C and the International Energy Agency.

The recommendation engine is browser-local and transparent. It converts a declared mission constitution into competing architectures, runs deterministic stress worlds, applies Proof Debt, authority, resilience, human-agency and reversibility constraints, and returns a recommended route with alternatives and switch conditions.

The AGI Club access layer reads ownership through the connected Ethereum wallet on Ethereum Mainnet. It supports direct ENS Registry ownership and direct ownership through the official Mainnet ENS Name Wrapper, then obtains a domain-bound non-transaction signature and performs a fresh ownership check before temporary admission.
