## The decision rule

A candidate becomes a **Mission-Dominant Successor** only when it creates preregistered positive net mission gain over the incumbent and the best credible alternative, after complete cost, proof, rights, risk, human burden, reversibility and governance integrity are counted.

Among passing candidates, GoalOS selects the architecture with the lowest justified **Total Institutional Burden**.
