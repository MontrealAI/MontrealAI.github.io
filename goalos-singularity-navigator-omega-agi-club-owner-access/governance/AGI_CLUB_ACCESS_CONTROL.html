AGI Club Direct-Owner Access Control

The gate accepts only one exact label.club.agi.eth name on Ethereum Mainnet.

Accepted

  • ENS Registry direct owner equals the connected wallet.
  • For a wrapped name, Registry owner equals the official Mainnet Name Wrapper, ownerOf(namehash) and getData(namehash).owner equal the connected wallet, and reported expiry remains current.

Rejected

  • Root club.agi.eth, nested names and other namespaces.
  • Resolver control, token approval, operator approval, manager rights, delegated authority, prior ownership or beneficial claims.
  • Expired wrapped names or unrecognized wrapper contracts.

Session

30 minutes; on-chain revalidation every five minutes and on tab/window focus; relock on account, chain or connection changes.
